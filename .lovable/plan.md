

# Dashboard Reinvention — From Transactional to Intelligence-First

After dropping in-app payments, the dashboards must shift from "running a register" to "running a relationship." The core mental model:

```text
OLD:  Dashboard = ledger      (revenue, occupancy, refunds)
NEW:  Dashboard = cockpit     (signals, intent, momentum, AI insights)
```

Each role gets a different cockpit:
- **Player** → a personal sports concierge
- **Owner** → a lead & reputation command center
- **Admin** → a marketplace health & growth console

---

## 1. Player Dashboard — "Your Sports Life"

Replace booking-centric stats with **identity, intent, and momentum**.

**Top hero (replaces the 4 numeric stat cards):**
- "Next Move" card — the single most relevant action right now (tonight's game, a saved venue with availability today, a teammate waiting on a reply). Generated by a small AI ranker.
- Streak + Level chip (already have XP/level in profiles) rendered as a pill, not a stat tile.

**Sections:**
1. **Upcoming Plans** — combined timeline of game RSVPs + sent booking inquiries (`booking_intents` where `user_id = me`), each with status: *Awaiting reply / Owner contacted / Confirmed / No response*. WhatsApp resend button on stalled inquiries.
2. **Your Squad** — quick access to teams, last-played-with players, and "ping on WhatsApp" shortcuts. Shows who's free this weekend (based on availability flag in profile, optional).
3. **Discover for you (AI)** — Lovable AI generated weekly: "3 fields you haven't tried that match your sport + skill + neighborhood." Refreshes every Monday.
4. **Your Sports DNA** — visual breakdown of sports played, favorite times, top venues. Feels like Spotify Wrapped, monthly.
5. **Achievements + Referrals** — keep, but compress into one row.

**Removed:** Total Revenue / refunds / payment intent IDs / Stripe status anywhere.

---

## 2. Owner Dashboard — "Lead & Reputation Cockpit"

The owner no longer needs revenue — they need **leads, conversion, and reputation**.

**Hero strip (4 KPIs):**
| KPI | Source |
|---|---|
| New Leads (7d) | `booking_intents` where venue_id ∈ my venues, status = clicked |
| Response Rate | % leads moved past `clicked` within 24h |
| Confirmed Bookings (self-reported) | `confirmed_booking` status |
| Reputation | avg `reviews.rating` + count |

**Sections:**
1. **Lead Inbox** (the new center of gravity) — list of every WhatsApp/SMS/Call handoff, with quick status buttons: *Confirmed ✓ / No-show / Lost*. Owner self-reports outcome in one tap. This becomes the dataset that powers everything.
2. **Today's Schedule** — manual bookings + self-reported confirmations, calendar style. No payment column.
3. **AI Coach** (Lovable AI, daily refresh) — 2–3 personalized nudges: *"You haven't replied to 3 leads from yesterday," "Your Friday 7pm slot has 4× more inquiries than supply — consider raising price or opening Court 2," "Add 2 more photos — listings with 5+ photos get 60% more leads on this platform."*
4. **Reputation Pulse** — newest reviews, sentiment summary by AI ("Players love the lighting; complaints about parking"), unanswered reviews CTA.
5. **Listing Health** — score (0–100) per venue: photos, description length, response rate, review freshness. Each item is fixable in one click.
6. **Reach & Visibility** — view counts, search appearances, click-to-WhatsApp ratio. (Trackable from existing `booking_intents` + page views if added.)

**Removed:** Stripe Connect banner, Total Revenue, payouts, occupancy %, refund flows.

---

## 3. Admin Dashboard — "Marketplace Health Console"

Admins lose the revenue lens but gain the **operator lens**: supply, demand, quality, growth.

**Hero KPIs (replace revenue):**
- Active supply (live venues), Active demand (DAU/WAU players), Lead volume (7d / 30d), Conversion rate (% of `clicked` → `confirmed_booking`), New signups by role.

**Sections:**
1. **Marketplace Pulse** — sparkline trends: leads/day, signups/day, listings/day, reviews/day. Spot regressions instantly.
2. **Supply ↔ Demand Heatmap** — by city × sport × time-of-day. Highlights deserts: "Tennis, Yerevan, Sat 6–8pm: 47 searches, 2 listings."
3. **Lead Inbox (global)** — already exists as Booking Leads tab; promote it. Add "owner not responding" filter for intervention.
4. **Quality Queue** — pending venues, candidate fields (AI-discovered), field submissions, reported chat messages — unified "things needing a human."
5. **AI Insights** (weekly, generated by Lovable AI from aggregated data) — "This week: tennis demand grew 18% in Gyumri but supply is flat. Consider outreach." Plain-English exec summary.
6. **Growth Loops** — referral activations, blog post traffic → signup conversion, top-performing acquisition channels.

**Removed:** Total Revenue card, payment-status filters on bookings table.

---

## 4. AI Layer (Lovable AI — google/gemini-3-flash-preview)

Three dedicated edge functions, each cached / scheduled to keep cost predictable:

| Function | Purpose | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| `player-insights` | Personal "Next Move" + weekly Sports DNA + venue recommendations | On dashboard load (cached 1h), weekly Sports DNA |
| `owner-coach` | Daily nudges, listing-health diagnostics, review sentiment | On dashboard load (cached 6h) |
| `admin-pulse` | Weekly marketplace exec summary + anomaly detection | Cron weekly, displayed as "AI Brief of the Week" |

All use structured output (tool calling) so the UI renders typed cards, not raw markdown blobs.

---

## 5. Visual & UX Direction

To keep the "high-end startup" feel without payment numbers carrying the weight:

- **Glanceable hero zone**: one bold statement card per dashboard ("3 leads need a reply", "Tonight: Football at 8pm", "Demand spike in Yerevan tennis") — large type, single accent color, no chartjunk.
- **Information density via cards, not tables**, on player/owner views. Tables stay for admin only.
- **Real momentum signals**: streaks, response time badges, reputation pulse — all data we already have or can derive from `booking_intents` and `reviews`.
- **No fake numbers**: every metric backed by a real query. Empty states tell the truth ("No leads yet — share your venue link") rather than fabricating activity.

---

## 6. Technical Notes

- Reuse `booking_intents` as the lead source of truth across all 3 dashboards.
- Add a `lead_outcome` enum tracking ('confirmed', 'no_show', 'lost', 'pending') updated by owner; powers conversion math everywhere.
- New hooks: `useLeads`, `useOwnerCoach`, `usePlayerNextMove`, `useAdminPulse`.
- New edge functions: `player-insights`, `owner-coach`, `admin-pulse` (Lovable AI gateway, structured output via tool calling, cached responses stored in a small `ai_insights_cache` table keyed by user_id + insight_type + date).
- Deprecate (don't delete) revenue/occupancy code paths so we can re-enable later if payments return.

---

## 7. Phased Rollout

1. **Phase 1 — Strip & Replace (low risk):** Remove revenue/Stripe widgets from all 3 dashboards. Replace stat cards with non-payment KPIs already queryable today.
2. **Phase 2 — Lead Inbox:** Build Owner Lead Inbox with self-reported outcomes; hook it into Admin's marketplace conversion math.
3. **Phase 3 — AI Layer:** Ship the three edge functions and the AI cards (Next Move, Owner Coach, Admin Pulse).
4. **Phase 4 — Polish:** Sports DNA, Listing Health score, Supply/Demand heatmap.

Each phase is shippable on its own and the dashboards stay coherent throughout.
