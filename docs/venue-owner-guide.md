# Getting your venue on Sportsbnb

A complete walkthrough — from empty account to your first paid booking, and how to earn more once you're running.

Read time: about 12 minutes. Setup time: about 30.

---

## Before you start

Have these to hand. Gathering them first turns a 30-minute setup into a 10-minute one.

- **Photos of your venue** — 5 to 10, taken in daylight. This matters more than anything else on this page.
- **Your bank account details** — IBAN and the account holder name exactly as your bank has it.
- **Your opening hours**, including any days you're closed.
- **Your hourly rate**, and whether it changes at weekends or in the evening.

---

## Step 1 — List your venue

**Owner dashboard → Venues → Add venue**

### The fields that decide whether you get booked

**Name.** Use the name people actually call it. "Nairi Arena" beats "Nairi Sports Complex LLC Field #2." If a player can't tell what it is at a glance, they scroll past.

**Photos.** This is the single biggest factor in whether your listing converts. A venue with clear photos gets booked several times more often than the same venue with one dark phone snap.

- Shoot in daylight, or under your floodlights if that's when people play
- First photo = the whole pitch or court, wide, from a corner
- Then: the surface close up, changing rooms, parking, the entrance
- No people's faces without their permission

**Address.** Pin the exact entrance on the map, not the middle of the building. Players arrive by taxi and a pin 100 metres off means a phone call to you.

**Sports.** Tick everything the surface genuinely supports. A 5-a-side pitch that also takes futsal should list both — you appear in twice as many searches.

**Description.** Three or four sentences. Answer what a player actually wants to know:

> Full-size artificial turf, floodlit until 23:00. Changing rooms with hot showers. Free parking for 12 cars. Balls and bibs available to rent. Two minutes from Barekamutyun metro.

Skip the marketing language. "The premier destination for football enthusiasts" tells a player nothing. "Floodlit until 23:00" gets you an evening booking.

**Price per hour.** See *Getting your price right* below — this is worth thinking about properly.

## Step 2 — Set your opening hours

**Owner dashboard → Hours**

Set your weekly schedule. Players can only book inside these windows, so this is your inventory.

Two things owners get wrong:

1. **Being too conservative.** If you'd accept a 07:00 booking from a group that asked, open 07:00. Empty early hours cost you nothing to list.
2. **Forgetting to block closed dates.** Holidays, maintenance, your own tournaments. Block them here — otherwise the system will happily sell an hour you can't honour, and you'll be the one making the apology.

---

## Step 3 — Add your bank details

**Owner dashboard → Earnings**

Three fields:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Method** | Bank transfer (IBAN) or Idram |
| **Destination** | Your `AM…` IBAN, or your Idram ID |
| **Account holder** | Your name or company name, **exactly** as your bank has it |

> **Get the holder name exactly right.** A mismatch between the name on the account and the name you type here is the single most common reason a transfer bounces. If your account is in your company's name, use the company name — not yours.

Your details are visible only to you. No player ever sees them.

**Do this before you take your first booking.** Your earnings accumulate whether or not this is filled in, but nothing can be sent to an account that doesn't exist yet.

---

## Step 4 — Choose your cancellation policy

**Owner dashboard → Policies**

| Policy | Free cancellation until | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Flexible** | 24 hours before | New listings, quiet slots, building reviews |
| **Moderate** | 48 hours before | Established venues with steady demand |
| **Strict** | 72 hours before | Prime slots that always sell out |

**Start flexible.** It is the single easiest thing you can do to get your first ten bookings. A player choosing between two similar venues will take the one where cancelling is easy, every time. Tighten it later, once you have reviews and a waiting list — not before.

The policy is locked to each booking at the moment it's made, so changing it never affects a booking someone has already paid for.

---

## Step 5 — Go live

Set your venue **active**. It now appears in search, on the map, and on the homepage.

Check your own listing as a player would: open it in a private browser window, pick a date, and look at the times offered. If something's wrong — a closed day showing as open, the wrong price — you'll see it immediately.

## How the money works

**You keep 100% of your listed price.**

Sportsbnb does not take a commission out of your rate. A separate service fee is charged to the **player**, on top of your price, and it covers card processing and running the platform.

A booking at 8,000 ֏:

```
Your rate                8,000 ֏   ← you
Service fee (player)     1,200 ֏   ← Sportsbnb
─────────────────────────────────
Player pays              9,200 ֏
```

You are paid **8,000 ֏**. Your rate is never reduced.

### What happens when someone books

1. A player picks a time and pays by card.
2. The slot is locked immediately — nobody else can take it.
3. You get a notification, and it appears in **Bookings**.
4. The amount is added to your balance in **Earnings**.
5. Once your balance passes **֏10,000**, it's paid out to your bank account — within 3 business days.

You never chase anyone for money and never handle cash. The player has already paid before they arrive.

### Cancellations and refunds

If a player cancels inside your policy window, the refund is handled automatically and the corresponding amount comes back out of your balance. If they cancel outside the window, you keep the booking. If you have to cancel on a player, they are refunded in full.

Every movement — earnings, refunds, payouts — is itemised in **Earnings**, so your balance is always explainable.

## Getting your price right

Your hourly rate is the most important number on your listing, and most owners set it once and never revisit it.

**Start at the local going rate, not below it.** Undercutting attracts price-shoppers who cancel. Matching the market and having better photos wins more bookings than being 10% cheaper.

**Then use the pricing rules** — see below — instead of moving your base rate around.

> **A note on very low rates:** rates under about 3,000 ֏/hour are difficult for the platform to process economically, because card networks charge a fixed amount per transaction regardless of size. If your natural rate is lower than that, consider selling in 2-hour blocks instead of single hours. It's better for you too — fewer changeovers, same revenue.

## Earning more

Everything below is already in your dashboard. These are the levers that separate a venue making a little from one making a lot.

### 1. Charge more when demand is higher

**Owner dashboard → Pricing**

Add rules for weekends, evenings, and peak hours. Your 19:00–22:00 weekday slots and your Saturday afternoons are worth more than Tuesday at 11:00 — price them that way.

A typical structure:

| When | Rate |
|---|---|
| Weekday daytime | base |
| Weekday evening (18:00–23:00) | base + 20–30% |
| Weekend | base + 20–40% |

The same slots sell out either way. This is the highest-return five minutes you will spend in the dashboard.

### 2. Discount your dead hours

The reverse of the above, and it's where the real growth is. An empty 10:00 Tuesday earns nothing. At 30% off it earns 70% of something, and it brings in players who then come back at full price.

Weekday mornings, early afternoons, and late nights are where new demand comes from — not from competing for the Saturday slots everyone already wants.

### 3. Rent equipment

**Owner dashboard → Equipment**

Balls, bibs, nets, rackets, floodlight extensions. Add them individually or as packages.

This is close to pure margin — you already own the equipment. A 1,000 ֏ ball-and-bibs package on a third of your bookings adds up faster than most owners expect, and it saves players a trip.

Package a few items together ("Match kit: 2 balls, 12 bibs, 2 nets") and price it slightly under the sum of its parts. Packages sell far better than a list of single items.

### 4. Put a booking button on your own channels

**Owner dashboard → Widget**

Copy the embed code onto your own website, or use the link in your Instagram bio and WhatsApp Business profile.

Players who already know you can then book and pay instantly instead of messaging you and waiting for a reply. Those messages are where bookings die — someone asks about Saturday, you reply two hours later, they've booked elsewhere.

### 5. Connect your calendar

**Owner dashboard → Integrations**

If you also take bookings by phone or in person, sync your calendar so those blocks show as unavailable here. Double-booking is the one mistake that reliably costs you a customer permanently.

### 6. Watch your numbers

**Owner dashboard → Analytics**

Look for:

- **Your busiest and emptiest hours** — the emptiest are your discount candidates
- **Days with zero bookings** — a pattern usually means your hours or price are wrong for that day
- **Repeat players** — your most valuable customers; consider a regular slot for them

## Common questions

**Do I pay anything to list?**
No. Listing is free, and no commission is deducted from your rate. The service fee is paid by the player on top of your price.

**When do I get paid?**
Once your balance passes ֏10,000, to the account you saved in Earnings, within 3 business days.

**What if a player doesn't turn up?**
The booking is already paid. Their cancellation window is set by your policy — outside it, the money is yours.

**Can I block time for my own use?**
Yes — block the date in **Hours**, or the individual slot in **Schedule**.

**Can I list more than one venue or court?**
Yes. Add each separately, or add courts under one venue if they're bookable independently.

**Can I change my price later?**
Any time. Existing bookings keep the price they were made at.

**A player wants to book 4 hours — is that possible?**
Yes, up to 8 hours in one booking.
